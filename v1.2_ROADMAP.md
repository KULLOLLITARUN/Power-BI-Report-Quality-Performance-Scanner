## 3. Phase 0: Real-World PBIP Audit

Before writing any new rule code, `pbiscan` must be benchmarked across a diverse corpus of real-world Power BI projects.

### 3.1 Project Corpus Categories
1. **Small Single-Table Models**: Flat tables, quick prototypes, basic visuals (<5 MB).
2. **Standard Star Schemas**: 1 Fact + 4-6 Dimensions, clean one-to-many active relationships (10–50 MB).
3. **Complex Enterprise Models**: Multiple facts, bridge tables, role-playing dimensions, bidirectional cross-filtering, extensive measure chains (50–500+ MB).
4. **Calculations-Heavy Models**: Financial reporting models with 100+ DAX measures, time-intelligence hierarchies, and semi-additive KPIs.

### 3.2 Finding Classification Taxonomy
For every finding produced during the audit, categorize as follows:

| Classification | Definition | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| **True Positive (TP)** | Legitimate anti-pattern, performance risk, or redundancy correctly detected. | Document frequency across models. |
| **False Positive (FP)** | Flagged object is intentional, valid, or falsely identified due to pattern limitations. | Refine regex/AST boundaries or calibrate confidence score down. |
| **False Negative (FN)** | Known model defect or high-risk pattern present in PBIP that was missed by scanner. | Identify missing signal; formulate candidate rule. |
| **Ambiguous (AMB)** | Detection is structurally correct, but operational impact depends on data volume/engine mode. | Ensure remediation prose and confidence percentage accurately hedge severity. |

## 4. Phase 1: Rule Calibration & Candidate Gates

### 4.1 Topology Rules Evaluation (`M006` / `M007`)
Topology infrastructure (`isolated_tables()`, `relationship_paths()`, `connected_components()`) was shipped in `canonical/` in v1.1.0. Candidate rules will only be promoted to the active rule table if they meet strict quality gates:

- **Candidate `M006` (Isolated / Disconnected Table)**:
  - *Criteria*: Must distinguish disconnected dimension/fact islands from legitimate disconnected calculation groups, what-if parameter tables, or DAX disconnected slicers (e.g. harvesting tables).
  - *Negative Fixture Required*: Models with What-If parameter tables and calculation groups must not trigger false alarms.
- **Candidate `M007` (Ambiguous Filter Path)**:
  - *Criteria*: Must distinguish active multi-path loops from valid star-schema multi-grain hierarchies.
  ### 4.3 Formal 10-Point Promotion Gate
A candidate rule may only be promoted from candidate infrastructure to the active diagnostic matrix when all 10 conditions are verified:
1. **Positive Golden Fixture**: Passes with clear structural defect detection.
2. **Negative Golden Fixture**: Passes with 0 false alarms on standard modeling patterns.
3. **Cycle/Safety Tests**: Verified termination on cyclic graph structures without infinite recursion.
4. **Real-World Corpus Evidence**: Documented occurrence in real-world PBIP models.
5. **TP/FP/AMB Classification**: Empirical classification recorded in `AUDIT_CLASSIFICATION_SHEET.md`.
6. **Explicit Confidence Justification**: Confidence rating mathematically/heuristically grounded.
7. **Reviewed Remediation Prose**: 4-part contract text reviewed for actionable clarity.
8. **151+ Zero Regression**: All existing unit and golden test suites remain green.
9. **Approved Severity & Scoring Deduction**: Exact deduction impact reviewed and approved.
10. **Behavioral Invariance**: Promotion does not silently alter existing rule evaluation.

### 4.4 Phase 1F Promotion Decisions
- **`M006` (Isolated Table)**: **DEFERRED**. Infrastructure and positive/negative fixtures verified. However, isolated tables may legitimately represent disconnected parameter tables, What-If tables, or DAX harvesting tables. Promotion is deferred pending real-world parameter classification.
- **`M007` (Ambiguous Path)**: **DEFERRED**. Infrastructure and cycle safety verified. However, multi-path topologies in complex schemas do not universally indicate defect behavior without engine context. Promotion is deferred.
- **Active Rule Matrix**: **Strictly locked at 11 rules** for v1.2.
